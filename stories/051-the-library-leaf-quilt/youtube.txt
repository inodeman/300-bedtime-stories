The Library Leaf Quilt | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Library Leaf Quilt is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Esme stitches a quilt of fallen leaves for the library bench so a gentle fox can read without wriggling on the cool wood. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness can be a soft seat offered to someone who stays a long while.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
