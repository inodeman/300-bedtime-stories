The Clock Song at Meadow Stop | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Clock Song at Meadow Stop is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Edgar wants the train at once, then sits with Papa, helps a child hold a ticket, and stands calmly when the whistle finally comes. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience can grow a minute at a time when your hands have a kind job.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
