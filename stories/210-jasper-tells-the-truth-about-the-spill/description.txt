Jasper Tells the Truth about the Spill is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Jasper spills the morning oat scoop and almost blames the wind. He tells the grown-up the truth instead. Together they compost the dusty oats and measure a fresh scoop for the chickens. Honesty makes the fix simple and keeps the morning kind. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Honesty helps people fix a mistake quickly and keeps trust strong.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
