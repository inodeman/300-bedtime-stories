The Note Slide is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Gus and Neri build a gentle cardboard chute from a window box to a porch basket so thank-you notes can slide down. The first note jams at a dent. With Papa's help they smooth the tube and widen the opening, and a note about borrowed chalk arrives with a papery whoosh. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A creative idea can be fixed with careful looking, and then it carries kind words.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
