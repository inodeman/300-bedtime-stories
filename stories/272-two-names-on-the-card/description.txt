Two Names on the Card is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Tomas draws pots and Joy draws flowers for Grandma Soraya. Tomas almost signs only his own name, then tells Joy the truth before the card is given. Both names go on the garden, and the cousins keep a reminder that shared work gets shared thanks. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Honesty keeps praise wide enough for everyone who helped.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
