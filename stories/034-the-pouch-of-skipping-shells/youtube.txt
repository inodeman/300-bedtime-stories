The Pouch of Skipping Shells | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Pouch of Skipping Shells is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Hattie has the best flat skipping shells in her pouch and decides to divide them so Grandpa and the lighthouse keeper can send skips across the calm water too. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing your best things lets more people step into the joy with you.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
