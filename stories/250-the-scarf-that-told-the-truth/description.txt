The Scarf That Told the Truth is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Sana knits a scarf and almost hides an extra loop that makes the edge wave. She tells Timo the truth. Together they ease the loop and later pick up a dropped stitch. The finished wonky scarf warms Grandpa's knees. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Honesty about a mistake makes it easier to mend and finish with pride.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
