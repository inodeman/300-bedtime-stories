The Counting Stones in the Bridge Pocket is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

The sea-blue cloth pocket on the bridge rail has gaped, and ten dotted counting stones have spilled into a dry groove. Orla gathers them and learns a simple knot from the bridge mender so the pocket will hold again. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Helping to put small things back makes a shared place welcoming again.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
