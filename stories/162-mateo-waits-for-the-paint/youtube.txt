Mateo Waits for the Paint | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Mateo Waits for the Paint is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Mateo paints a moon and a river on his paper lantern, but the paint is still wet when the walk begins to gather. He waits until the colors dry, then joins the calm procession with a lantern that still shows its picture. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience lets good colors stay bright until the right moment arrives.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
