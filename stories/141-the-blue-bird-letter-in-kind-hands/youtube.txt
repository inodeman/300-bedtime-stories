The Blue Bird Letter in Kind Hands | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Blue Bird Letter in Kind Hands is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Camila buys a bird stamp for Grandma, then walks Mrs. Adeyemi's waiting letter home by hand so a high step does not keep the news away. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness should be careful as well as willing.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
