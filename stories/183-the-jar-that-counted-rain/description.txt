The Jar That Counted Rain is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Fatima and Gus wonder how much rain fell in the night. They make a simple rain gauge from a clear jar. A second jar under a leafy bush collects less, and they discover that where you put the jar changes what it tells you. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Curiosity means looking closely, asking questions, and checking your idea in more than one place.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
