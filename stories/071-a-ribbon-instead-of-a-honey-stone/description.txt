A Ribbon Instead of a Honey Stone is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Thick honey will not pour onto the cinnamon rolls until Yara, Sable, and Lewis warm the jar and wait, then share the golden ribbons. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Warmth and waiting can turn a stubborn lump into a gentle ribbon.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
