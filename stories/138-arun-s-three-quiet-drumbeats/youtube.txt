Arun's Three Quiet Drumbeats | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Arun's Three Quiet Drumbeats is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At the night market a drum keeper invites children to tap a quiet pattern, and shy Arun steps forward to play three gentle beats. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can sound as soft as three gentle beats when you decide to begin.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
