Hugh and the Wide Blue Sky | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Hugh and the Wide Blue Sky is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Hugh begins a backdrop sky alone, then invites Victor and Mei to paint bands of blue with him. Their birds, cloud, and hill make one wide sky for the puppet show. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A shared sky has room for every hand that helps to paint it.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
