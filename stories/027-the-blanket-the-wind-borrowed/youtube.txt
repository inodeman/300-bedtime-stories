The Blanket the Wind Borrowed | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Blanket the Wind Borrowed is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Kira's wide striped picnic blanket keeps leaping up in the dune wind until she, Nana, and Uncle each hold a part and weigh the corners down together. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Teamwork can turn a flappy mess into a steady place to sit.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
