The Cinnamon Truth is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Habiba adds too much cinnamon to Grandma Lotta's honey rice and hides the jar. The pot tastes too strong. Habiba tells the truth, and together they balance the rice, then write the recipe on a card so the next memory starts with an honest question. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Telling the truth helps a mistake become something you can fix.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
