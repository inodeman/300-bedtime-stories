Leaves Left on the Stem | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Leaves Left on the Stem is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Una wants a huge handful of mint so the soup will taste louder. Grandma Soraya and cousin Amina show her how to snip a few sprigs and leave the growing tips. The soup smells wonderful, and the plant stays leafy for another day. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Nature stays generous when we take only enough.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
