Juniper and the Second Lantern is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

On the town lantern walk, Juniper sees the canal holding copies of the lights and hears the bridge gently returning the flute's notes. She crosses twice, slowly, just to watch and listen. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Wonder grows when you look closely and let the world show you its quiet copies.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
