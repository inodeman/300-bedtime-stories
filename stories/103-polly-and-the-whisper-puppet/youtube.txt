Polly and the Whisper Puppet | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Polly and the Whisper Puppet is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Polly wants to welcome friends with a mouse puppet, but her voice hides. She and Jalen practice a gentle duet on the rug, and Polly speaks her line clearly for a small audience. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can be a clear small voice that stays to the end of the line.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
