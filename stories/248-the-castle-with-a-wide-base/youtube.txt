The Castle with a Wide Base | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Castle with a Wide Base is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Oona and Piotr build a cardboard castle. A tower box slides off, and Oona feels fluttery, then agrees to one new way. Tape and a wider hug make the lookout stay. They finish a door, a flag, and a welcome card, proud in a steady way. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can be a small nod and one more try after something tips.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
