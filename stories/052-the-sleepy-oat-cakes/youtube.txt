The Sleepy Oat Cakes | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Sleepy Oat Cakes is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Ivo lets oat-cake batter rest until it is sleepy and thick, using the wait to prepare a kind table for a fox friend. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience gives good things time to become ready.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
