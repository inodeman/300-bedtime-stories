The Paper Sun on the Low Stage is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Rosie is asked to hang the class's paper sun on the low stage wall, a job she has never done in front of everyone, and she does it steadily, one pin at a time. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage is trying a new part even when your hands feel brand new at it.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
