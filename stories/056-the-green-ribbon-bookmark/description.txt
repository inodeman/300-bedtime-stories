The Green Ribbon Bookmark is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Henrik finds a green ribbon bookmark in his basket and walks it back to the fox who needs it for a fern page. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Honesty returns what is not yours while the day is still kind.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
