The Sky in the Oak-Root Cup | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Sky in the Oak-Root Cup is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Wren finds a rain-filled hollow in an oak root that reflects the sky and wants to carry the water home, then chooses to leave it so the moss and the picture can stay. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Wonder sometimes means leaving a beautiful thing where it is.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
