The Window Game with Sora is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Mireia shares her window with shy Sora, and they take turns naming red doors, baskets, and hedges until the stub list holds both of their words. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing a view can start a friendship before the stop arrives.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
