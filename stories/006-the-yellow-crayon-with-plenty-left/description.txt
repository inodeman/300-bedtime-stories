The Yellow Crayon with Plenty Left is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Juniper and Nico both need the last bright yellow crayon for a class mural, and Juniper offers a plan to share it stroke by stroke so the sun and the wheat can both be colored. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing a turn can make one small thing feel like enough.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
