Questions for the Photo Album is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Joy wants to race through every old photo. Grandpa Ivan invites her questions and lingers on yellow curtains, a cup drummer, and a balcony visit. Joy and cousin Omar add a drawing of their own afternoon to an empty pocket in the album. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Curiosity grows kinder when we stay long enough to listen.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
