Seven Counts Between a Far Rumbling | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Seven Counts Between a Far Rumbling is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Tamsin hears distant thunder, stays inside with Mom and Anita, counts the gap after the flicker, and tells them when a rumble still startles her. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Brave and close can be the same thing when you stay in and say how you feel.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
