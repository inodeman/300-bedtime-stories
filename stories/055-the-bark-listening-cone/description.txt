The Bark Listening Cone is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Zuri rolls a bark listening cone and writes the different sounds of oaks, ferns, moss, and the bridge on her paper map. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Curiosity listens closely enough to learn the wood's many quiet voices.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
