Quentin's Lampmoon in the Window | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Quentin's Lampmoon in the Window is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Quentin paints a round Lampmoon, tapes it beside the real moon, and hides a smaller one inside the cardboard tube as a surprise for Grandma. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A homemade light can glow beside the real sky without needing to be the only one.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
