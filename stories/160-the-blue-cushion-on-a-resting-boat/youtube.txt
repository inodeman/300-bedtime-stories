The Blue Cushion on a Resting Boat | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Blue Cushion on a Resting Boat is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Wendy saves the dry blue cushion for visiting cousin Bea, narrates the biscuit tin and the canal, and lets the friendship begin while the boat stays tied. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Friendship can start with a saved seat, a clear choice, and room for quiet.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
