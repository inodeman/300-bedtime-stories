Four Pats and a Cool Drink | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Four Pats and a Cool Drink is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Stig and Inez dig, place, pat, mulch, and water three seedlings together so no plant gets a bigger drink than the others. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Teamwork is a pattern of digging, placing, patting, and sharing the drink.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
