Kwame and the Slow Bean is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Kwame's bean is slower than Ruth's. He wants to hurry it, then chooses to keep the towel damp and wait. A pale root appears when he gives the seed time. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Waiting can be a kind of care, and quiet growing still counts.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
