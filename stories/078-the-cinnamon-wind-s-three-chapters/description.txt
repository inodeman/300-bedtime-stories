The Cinnamon Wind's Three Chapters is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

A cinnamon wind tugs Amira, Karim, and Yara through the market until they name warm crust, orange oil, and spice bark and follow the trail. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Slow sniffing can read a recipe that sun and trees already wrote.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
