Rafi and the Pebble Path | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Rafi and the Pebble Path is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

After rain, the way to the chickens' dust bath is slick. Rafi invites friends to lay a path of small pebbles. Each person carries only a few stones, Button keeps a quiet watch, and the chickens use the path when they wish. Teamwork stays light and willing. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Teamwork turns many light efforts into one sturdy kindness.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
