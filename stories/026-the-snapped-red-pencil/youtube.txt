The Snapped Red Pencil | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Snapped Red Pencil is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Jonah snaps the keeper's red pencil while marking the tide chart and nearly hides the pieces in a shell dish, then tells the truth so the boat notes stay clear and useful. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Telling the truth quickly keeps a small mistake from turning into a muddle.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
