The Green Tiles That Were Meant to Be Gray | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Green Tiles That Were Meant to Be Gray is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Ilaria swaps two glaze cups, so her little bridge-shaped test tiles come out leaf-green instead of water-gray. She almost hides them, then tells the potter kindly. The green tiles become orchard leaves on the mural plan instead of a wasted mistake. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Telling the truth early lets a mix-up become useful instead of hidden.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
