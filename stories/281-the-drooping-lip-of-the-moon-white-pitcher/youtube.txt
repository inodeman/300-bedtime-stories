The Drooping Lip of the Moon-White Pitcher | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Drooping Lip of the Moon-White Pitcher is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Yara is making a small moon-white cream pitcher for the fountain flower cups, but the pouring lip keeps drooping. She waits through three kind chimes, mists the clay, and shapes the lip slowly so the water pours in one clear line. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Waiting a little and trying again with a slower hand can shape what hurry keeps bending.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
