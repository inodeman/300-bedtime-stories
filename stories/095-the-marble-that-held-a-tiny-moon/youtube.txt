The Marble That Held a Tiny Moon | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Marble That Held a Tiny Moon is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Bibi discovers a lamp and a moon inside a glass marble, then looks at drops and leaves with Mama without startling the cricket. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Careful looking lets a question grow without breaking what you love.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
