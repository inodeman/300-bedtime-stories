Hills Rain and a Walking Tree | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Hills Rain and a Walking Tree is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Fabio and Gul block-print a picnic cloth with hills, leaves, and windy rain. A half leaf becomes a walking tree, and a sleeve smear becomes a pond. They finish the cloth and eat a careful snack on their made-up landscape. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Creativity grows when you keep a surprise and give it a job.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
