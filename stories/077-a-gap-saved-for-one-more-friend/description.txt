A Gap Saved for One More Friend is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Tessa arrives with orange peels and nowhere to sit, so Reina and Pavel move baskets and jars until the table has a gap and a welcome. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Friendship makes room when a full table still has one more friend at the door.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
