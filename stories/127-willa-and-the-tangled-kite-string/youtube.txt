Willa and the Tangled Kite String | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Willa and the Tangled Kite String is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Willa's kite string tangles on the hill and she wants to yank it free, but she sits and loosens each loop until the kite can rise. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience untangles what hurrying only tightens.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
