Penelope and the Extra Apple is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

After a light picnic, Penelope finds a second apple in her pocket. She almost keeps it as a secret snack, then tells the truth and brings it back to share. The apple is sliced for everyone, and Penelope learns that honesty protects the joy of a shared day. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Honesty about a small hidden thing keeps sharing real.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
