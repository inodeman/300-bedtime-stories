The Tipped Saucer Under the Pear Tree is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Ksenia notices that a clay rain saucer under a young pear tree has tipped, so water runs away from the roots while neighboring trees still glisten. With the orchard keeper she levels the saucer and tucks leaves around the roots like a blanket. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Noticing what a young tree needs is a quiet way of caring for nature.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
