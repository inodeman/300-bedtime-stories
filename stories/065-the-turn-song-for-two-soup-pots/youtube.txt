The Turn Song for Two Soup Pots | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Turn Song for Two Soup Pots is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

When the oven bell and both soups call at the same minute, Emile, Nkechi, and Tomas make a turn song so each job gets a clear hand. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Taking turns keeps a busy kitchen gentle when everything is ready at once.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
