The Bucket with the Clear Bottom | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Bucket with the Clear Bottom is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Paloma cannot see the starfish clearly through the ripples, so she lowers her wooden bucket with the glass bottom and studies the pool without ever lifting the animals out. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Curiosity is gentlest when you look closely and let wild things stay where they are.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
