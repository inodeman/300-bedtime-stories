The Dented Acorn Cap | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Dented Acorn Cap is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Felix returns a borrowed acorn-cap button he dented and helps the library fox smooth it instead of hiding it. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Honesty means bringing a mistake back with a clear voice.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
