Circles for Cookies | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Circles for Cookies is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Qadir and Luna are packing cookies for neighbors. Qadir quietly gives his favorite plate two extra cookies, then tells Luna the truth. They make a cardboard board of circles so each cookie has one home, recount honestly, and deliver even shares, with a thank-you plate for Nana. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Honesty means telling the truth about an uneven share and then making it fair.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
