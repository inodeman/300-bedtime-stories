Gideon and the Dog's Quiet Hello | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Gideon and the Dog's Quiet Hello is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Button the dog loves to greet everyone with big wiggles. The chickens prefer a calm yard. Gideon teaches Button a quiet hello: sit, soft eyes, one gentle wag, and wait. The chickens come closer on their own, and friendship feels easier for everyone. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Friendship grows when we learn the kind of greeting a friend can enjoy.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
