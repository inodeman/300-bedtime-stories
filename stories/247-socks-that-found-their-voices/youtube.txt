Socks That Found Their Voices | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Socks That Found Their Voices is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Marco and Nura turn two socks into a baker puppet and a gardener puppet. Mama sews the button eyes. A tumble and runaway pretend berries become part of the play, solved with a napkin ramp. They rest the finished puppets on the sill. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Creativity can turn an ordinary scrap into a story with room for surprises.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
