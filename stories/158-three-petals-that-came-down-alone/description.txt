Three Petals That Came Down Alone is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Uma wants a basket of hill flowers, then draws them instead and keeps only three petals that had already fallen. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Loving a place can mean leaving its flowers where they grow.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
