The Knot in the Soft Rope is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

A soft boat rope cinches into a tight knot, and Bodhi, Auntie, and Grandpa each hold a loop until the loose end slips free in time for a tidy tie-up. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Teamwork means each person holds a different part until the whole thing loosens.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
