Arun Follows the Braided Sound | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Arun Follows the Braided Sound is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Arun follows a gentle bell through the festival lanes and finds it is not one instrument but a wind chime, a humming neighbor, and a distant flute braided together. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Wonder appears when you listen long enough to hear separate sounds become one.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
