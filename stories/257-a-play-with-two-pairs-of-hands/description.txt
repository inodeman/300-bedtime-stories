A Play with Two Pairs of Hands is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Nura and Oona use sock puppets to tell about a bridge and a pretend river. When a plank is placed in the deep part, they pause, use real voices, and choose a better spot together. The picnic crosses, and the puppets rest finished on the sill. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Teamwork is planning together and pausing when you disagree.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
