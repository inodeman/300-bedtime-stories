The First Loop | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The First Loop is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Timo is nervous about knitting a first loop. Agnes shows slowly without taking his needles. A dropped stitch is picked up, the mark of the repair stays, and the finished wonky blue scarf goes on the reading chair as a true, warm gift. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can be a tiny first try, and a second try in the same minute.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
