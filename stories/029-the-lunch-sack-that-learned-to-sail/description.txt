The Lunch Sack That Learned to Sail is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Briony has no cloth for her toy boat's sail, so she paints a plain paper lunch sack with berry juice and shell stamps until the breeze can push her little wooden boat. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A plain scrap can become something wonderful if you give it a new idea.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
