Nola and the Puppet Garden is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Nola cannot wave the mouse and hold the vine at once. Stefan handles the paper garden, Abena keeps the shaker cues, and Nola speaks for the mouse, so the little show works. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Teamwork lets each person do one job well so the whole story can shine.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
