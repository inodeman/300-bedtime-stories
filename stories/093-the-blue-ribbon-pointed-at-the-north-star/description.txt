The Blue Ribbon Pointed at the North Star is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Parvati feels her voice hide, then uses a blue ribbon to point out the North Star and tell her family that it stays so people can find their way. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A brave voice can be small and still say something true.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
