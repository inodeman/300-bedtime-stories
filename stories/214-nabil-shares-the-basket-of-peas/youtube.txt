Nabil Shares the Basket of Peas | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Nabil Shares the Basket of Peas is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Nabil picks a basket of peas and wants them all for a snack. Remembering that ducks and chickens enjoy peas, he divides the basket into three fair parts. He learns that sharing can follow a plan so no one is left out and nothing is wasted. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing feels fair when everyone, including you, gets a thoughtful portion.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
