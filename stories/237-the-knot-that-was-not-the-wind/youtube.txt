The Knot That Was Not the Wind | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Knot That Was Not the Wind is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Quinlan almost blames the breeze for a wobble, then admits he twisted the string and helps Jovan wind it clear. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Saying the true part lets a tangle become a clear string.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
