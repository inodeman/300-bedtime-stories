The Oak Leaf Platter | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Oak Leaf Platter is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Hassan brings a wide oak-leaf platter of berry scones to the picnic clearing and shares the middle scone with three gentle foxes. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing the best piece can make a picnic feel bigger.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
