The Brass Fish Clip on the Book Barge | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Brass Fish Clip on the Book Barge is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

On the book barge, wind keeps closing the map-book page that shows the hill orchard because the brass fish page-clip is missing. Anouk follows a tiny jingle to a barrel of spare cushions and finds the clip caught on a thread. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Curious listening can find what a hurry would miss.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
