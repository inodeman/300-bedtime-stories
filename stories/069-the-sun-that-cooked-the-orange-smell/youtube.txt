The Sun That Cooked the Orange Smell | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Sun That Cooked the Orange Smell is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Tessa and Amira dry orange peels in the Saturday sun, compare them with shade peels, and tuck the sunny curls into honey jars. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Noticing and waiting let nature's brightness become something the table can share.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
