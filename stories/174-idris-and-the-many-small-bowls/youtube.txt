Idris and the Many Small Bowls | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Idris and the Many Small Bowls is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Idris helps serve his uncle's lentil soup at the harvest festival. He chooses small bowls for everyone, including himself last, so the pot welcomes more neighbors. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing a big pot means giving many people enough instead of keeping the most.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
