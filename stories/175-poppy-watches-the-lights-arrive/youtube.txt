Poppy Watches the Lights Arrive | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Poppy Watches the Lights Arrive is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Poppy sits on her front step and watches neighbors light their lanterns one by one down the street. The lights arrive like a slow song, and she joins the pattern with her own lamp at the right moment. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Wonder can be as simple as watching one small light follow another.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
