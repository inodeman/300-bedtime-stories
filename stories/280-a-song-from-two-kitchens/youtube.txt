A Song From Two Kitchens | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

A Song From Two Kitchens is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Chiara's home clap and Deepak's spoon tap collide when they try to play at once. They feel wobbly, then teach each other slowly and blend a new pattern with room for both. Each cousin keeps a copy so the same gentle song can live in two kitchens. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Friendship has room for two ways of doing something.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
