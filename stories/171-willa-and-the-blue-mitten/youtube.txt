Willa and the Blue Mitten | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Willa and the Blue Mitten is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

During the lantern walk, Willa finds a blue mitten on a garden wall. She carries it carefully until she finds Mrs. Pell, whose hand was cold, and returns it with a smile. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness means noticing what was dropped and taking the trouble to return it.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
