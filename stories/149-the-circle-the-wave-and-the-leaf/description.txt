The Circle, the Wave, and the Leaf is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Lars invents simple flag marks and ties hello, wind, and thank-you flags to the balloon basket rail without touching the ropes. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A clear homemade mark can say hello to a whole fair.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
