A Soft Hello for the Kitten is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Tova and Wynn welcome a new kitten who hides when they rush and speak loudly. They build a soft cardboard nook and a slow ribbon toy. They wait without grabbing. The kitten comes out to the warm spot, and they learn that a gentle hello takes time. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience lets a new friend come closer when the room stays gentle and unhurried.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
