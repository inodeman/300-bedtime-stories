The Turn Ribbon Across the Rug is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Luca lays a turn ribbon so he and Papa trade the telescope after one cricket song, then splits the biscuits and the last milk the same fair way. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A fair turn is a promise that keeps sharing cozy.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
