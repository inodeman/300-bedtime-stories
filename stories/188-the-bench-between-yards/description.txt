The Bench Between Yards is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Paz and Qadir make a shared tool bench from a sturdy crate so their string, tape, and clothespins have homes. They both grab the tape and tangle it. A turn card and labeled cups fix the problem, and together they steady a wobbly plant stake for the yard. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Shared tools stay useful when everyone takes a turn and puts each thing back.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
