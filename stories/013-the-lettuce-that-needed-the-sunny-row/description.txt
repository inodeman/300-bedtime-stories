The Lettuce That Needed the Sunny Row is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Ollie sees his class about to plant lettuce in a shady corner and speaks up so the seedlings can go in the sunny row where they will grow well. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can be a clear, kind voice when a plan needs changing.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
