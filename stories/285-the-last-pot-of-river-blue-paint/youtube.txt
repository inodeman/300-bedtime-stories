The Last Pot of River-Blue Paint | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Last Pot of River-Blue Paint is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Cressida has the last small pot of river-blue paint for the community mural. Both the painted ferry window and a friend's sky puddle need blue. She shares the pot and uses a thin brush so each place gets enough. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing a small amount, used with care, can finish two kind parts of the same picture.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
