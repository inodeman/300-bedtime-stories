The Quiet Row of Radish Seeds is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Lila wants to uncover her radish seeds to see if they are growing, then learns to water them gently and wait until the first green tips appear on their own. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience lets small things grow in their own time.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
