The Hello Folded in Noura's Pocket | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Hello Folded in Noura's Pocket is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Noura climbs the known path with her words folded small, then sits with Jules and Amira and offers a clear lookout hello. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A small voice can come out of a pocket when friends make room for it.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
