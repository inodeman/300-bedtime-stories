Faye and the Color Song is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Faye paints while Dimitri and Polly play color patterns on shakers. Rests become white space, taps become dots, and a listening spiral ties the fair painting together. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Creativity can mix colors and sounds until a picture and a song share one shape.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
