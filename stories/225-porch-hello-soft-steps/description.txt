Porch Hello, Soft Steps is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Elif and Miren hang shells and a moon bead, space them so they do not crowd, and keep both names for the porch chime. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A friendly sound belongs to both makers when nobody has to win the name.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
