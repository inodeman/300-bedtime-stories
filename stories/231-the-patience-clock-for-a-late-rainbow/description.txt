The Patience Clock for a Late Rainbow is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Kesia wants the rainbow to hurry, so Rina helps her sip, look, and keep her hands quiet until the bow stands over the far trees. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A rainbow needs a moment for sun and rain to meet.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
