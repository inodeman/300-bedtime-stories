Kenji and the Fallen Leaf | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Kenji and the Fallen Leaf is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Kenji and Faye make chalk rubbings of bark and a fallen leaf, return the leaf to the soil, and compare its veins with the sprouts in the library window jars. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Caring for nature means noticing closely and leaving living things whole.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
