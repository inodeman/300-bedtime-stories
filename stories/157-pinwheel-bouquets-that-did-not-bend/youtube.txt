Pinwheel Bouquets That Did Not Bend | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Pinwheel Bouquets That Did Not Bend is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Tilde, Otto, and Mama pack paper pinwheels into smaller bouquets with paper collars, trade jobs when the hurry rises, and deliver them unbent. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A second try works better when nobody is blamed and everybody has a job.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
