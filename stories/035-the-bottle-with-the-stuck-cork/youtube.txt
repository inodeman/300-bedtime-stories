The Bottle with the Stuck Cork | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Bottle with the Stuck Cork is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Yusuf finds the keeper's corked question bottle on the shell line, eases the sandy cork loose, and checks a tide pool to answer the riddle rolled inside. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Curiosity asks a careful question and then goes looking, gently, for the answer.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
