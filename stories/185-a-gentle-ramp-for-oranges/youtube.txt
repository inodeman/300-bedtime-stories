A Gentle Ramp for Oranges | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

A Gentle Ramp for Oranges is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Jamila and Kostas want to bring oranges to a neighbor, but their wagon stops at a low step. They build a ramp. The first ramp is too steep and the fruit rolls. They make the ramp longer and gentler, with Papa checking that it will not slip, and the oranges arrive safely. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A kind errand works best when we make the path gentle enough for what we carry.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
