The Wide Strap of the Peach Basket | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Wide Strap of the Peach Basket is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Bima wants to carry a tall peach basket with a wide leather strap down the orchard path, but the strap feels too big and he worries the fruit will bump. With the orchard keeper beside him, he carries a shorter row, one peach at a time, and finds the basket steady. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can be a slow walk beside a grown-up, one peach at a time.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
