Felix Waits for the Ferry Bell is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Felix wants to run to the front of the ferry before it is ready, waits for the deck bell instead, and enjoys a calm walk to the best view. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience keeps a busy moment gentle for you and for everyone nearby.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
