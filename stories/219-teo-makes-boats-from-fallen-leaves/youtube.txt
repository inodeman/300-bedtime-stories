Teo Makes Boats from Fallen Leaves | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Teo Makes Boats from Fallen Leaves is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Teo wants to make something new for a quiet pond afternoon. He uses fallen leaves for little boats and a loose grass ring Bramble can wear or shake off. The ducks nudge the boats if they wish, and Teo learns that creative play can be gentle and temporary. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Creativity is especially kind when what we make can return gently to the world.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
