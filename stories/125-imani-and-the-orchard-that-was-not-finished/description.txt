Imani and the Orchard That Was Not Finished is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Imani thinks the orchard is used up after the baskets are full, then slows down and notices dew, moths, low fruit, and evening lights in the grass, filling her empty sketch with wonder. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Wonder grows when you look again at a place you thought had nothing left to show you.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
