The Paper Sun Caught in the Rosemary is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At noon the clock keeper lowers a basket of folded paper suns so children can count the kind chimes. One sun is caught on a rosemary sprig in the window box. Nassim notices, tells the keeper, and folds a spare so the count still has enough. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness can be speaking up when you see that someone, even a paper sun, has been left out.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
