The Dented Watering Can | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Dented Watering Can is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

On a hot afternoon Asha notices Mr. Okonkwo's seedlings drooping while he is at the market, and she uses the dented watering can he left for her to give them a gentle drink. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness is giving care to something that is not only yours.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
