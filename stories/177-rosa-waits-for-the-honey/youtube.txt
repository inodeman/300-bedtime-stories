Rosa Waits for the Honey | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Rosa Waits for the Honey is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Rosa wants a taste of festival honey right away, but the tasting table has a calm line. She waits, helps pass spoons, and finds the honey tastes better when her turn comes unhurried. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience at a shared table lets everyone taste without a rush.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
