The Wooden Spoon Orchestra | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Wooden Spoon Orchestra is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Rain keeps Malik, Pema, and Vijay indoors, so they build a band from pots, wooden spoons, and a rice tin. A noisy muddle becomes a layered soup song with a brave quiet part. They name the band The Whisper Tin and leave a picture map for the next rainy visit. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Creativity can turn ordinary kitchen things into shared music.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
