The Paper Breeze is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Kostas and Fatima want to carry honey bread to a neighbor, but the pan is still warm. They invent a paper fan on a stick to stir the air. The first fan is too small, so they make a bigger folded one. They still wait until Papa says the pan is cool enough to touch, and then the bread goes out golden and safe. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Creativity can cool a problem, and patience still tells us when something is ready.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
