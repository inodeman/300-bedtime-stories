Two Tubes and a Striped Sock | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Two Tubes and a Striped Sock is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Idris ties two cardboard tubes together with a striped sock so he and Grandma can look at the moon together and share the same small oh. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Friendship is looking at the same moment side by side.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
