Planks Over the Puddle | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Planks Over the Puddle is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Rita and Soren want to carry cookies to Grandpa's porch, but a wide shallow puddle covers the lane. With his help they lay planks across it. The first plank rocks, so they add a second plank and flat stones at each end. The cookies stay dry, and they cross together. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Teamwork can make a simple, steady path when everyone goes slowly and checks the wobbly parts.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
