Hana and the Resting Pot is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Hana helps carry rice pudding to the festival supper but wants to taste it early. She lets the pot rest, sets out bowls, and discovers the grains are creamy only because they had time. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience gives good food the quiet time it needs to become ready.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
