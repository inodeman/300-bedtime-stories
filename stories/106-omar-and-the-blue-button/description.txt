Omar and the Blue Button is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Omar finds a blue button that would suit a puppet coat. He tells Cora he wanted it, then returns it to her cardigan and makes an honest paper button instead. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Saying what you want, and still returning what is not yours, keeps trust strong.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
