The Bell at the Garden Gate is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Neri and Oscar visit a neighbor who cannot hear soft knocks while working in the back garden. They hang a small bell by the gate. At first it stays quiet because it is still wrapped in cloth. They uncover it, ring a friendly hello, and share lemonade among the herbs. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A small, clear hello can help a neighbor know that kindness is at the gate.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
