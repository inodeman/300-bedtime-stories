Abena and the Fair Hello | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Abena and the Fair Hello is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Abena is meant to welcome friends at the fair, and her voice feels tucked away. Tariq helps her practice from one person to the banner to the courtyard, and she speaks clearly. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can be a steady sentence spoken in the sunshine with a friend nearby.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
