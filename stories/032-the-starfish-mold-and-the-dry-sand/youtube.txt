The Starfish Mold and the Dry Sand | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Starfish Mold and the Dry Sand is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Nico's starfish-shaped sand mold keeps crumbling in the dry upper sand until he waits for the damp tide line and packs it slowly enough to stand. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience finds the right dampness and lets careful work hold together.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
