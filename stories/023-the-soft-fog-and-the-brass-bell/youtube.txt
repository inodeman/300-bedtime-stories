The Soft Fog and the Brass Bell | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Soft Fog and the Brass Bell is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

When soft fog hides the cove, Saffron feels nervous about the big brass bell, then pulls the rope so the little wooden boats can hear where the friendly lighthouse stands. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can be one clear, careful sound when someone needs a guide.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
