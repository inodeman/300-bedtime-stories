The Breezy Bridge Satchel | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Breezy Bridge Satchel is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Priya carries a satchel of library leaves across the breezy rope footbridge, pausing to tuck each page safely in. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage is a calm hold and a song that does not rush.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
