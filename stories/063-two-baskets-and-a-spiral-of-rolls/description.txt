Two Baskets and a Spiral of Rolls is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Karim opens his carefully packed cinnamon rolls and Chioma opens her soup and honey so they and Reina can eat together without fear of running out. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing what you were saving can turn two full baskets into one easy meal.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
