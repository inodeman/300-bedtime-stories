Bea and the Creek Pebble Crown is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Bea collects the shiniest pebbles for a crown and then shares them with a visiting child who has an empty tin, so they can each make a pattern on the bank. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing the best pieces can make two smaller joys instead of one lonely pile.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
