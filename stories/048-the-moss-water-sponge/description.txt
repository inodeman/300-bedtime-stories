The Moss Water Sponge is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Arlo carries a moss water-sponge from the fern hollow to give a careful drink to a young oak with dusty roots. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Caring for nature means sharing water without taking too much.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
