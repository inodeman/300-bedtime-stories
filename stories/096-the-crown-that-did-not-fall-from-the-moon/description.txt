The Crown That Did Not Fall from the Moon is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Soren almost claims his paper crown fell from the moon, then tells Grandpa he cut and glued it himself and keeps the true story. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A true story about what you made is stronger than a shiny pretend one.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
