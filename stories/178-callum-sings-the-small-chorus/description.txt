Callum Sings the Small Chorus is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Callum knows only the chorus of the town's gentle evening song. He chooses to sing it softly beside Aunt Mae, and his small voice helps the circle stay together. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can sound like a small voice joining in at the right moment.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
