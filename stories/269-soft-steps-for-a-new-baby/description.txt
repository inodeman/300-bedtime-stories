Soft Steps for a New Baby is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Chiara wants to play loud blocks, but new baby cousin Rafael is asleep. She chooses quiet jobs instead, smoothing a blanket, drawing a picture, and humming a whisper song. The calm afternoon makes her feel included, tender, and proud. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness can be busy even when the room must stay quiet.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
