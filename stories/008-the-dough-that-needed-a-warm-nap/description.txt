The Dough That Needed a Warm Nap is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Ellis wants to bake the bread at once, but the dough has not risen, so he learns to give it a warm rest until it is ready to become a proper loaf. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience gives quiet work the time it needs to finish.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
