The Nap Time Basket | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Nap Time Basket is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Fatima and Oscar want to help when a new baby sibling is napping. They make a low basket cart of folded cloths and a paper sign that asks for soft feet. The cart squeaks, so they pad the wheels. Mama hangs a slow paper mobile aside from the crib, and the baby keeps resting. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience and gentle hands help a baby rest and help a family stay calm.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
