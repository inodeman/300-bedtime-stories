Stefan and the Leaning Sprout | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Stefan and the Leaning Sprout is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Stefan and Nola watch seed jars in a patch of library sun, compare them with a fallen leaf, and learn that stems lean toward light. They leave the living plants unpicked. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Nature is a neighbor to notice, not a thing to grab.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
