The Polite Breeze and the Red Kite is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Anita wants to know how a kite rises, so she and Luca watch the bush leaves, tip the cloth, and let a polite breeze do the lifting. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Watching small signs and asking questions keeps a wonder from flying away too fast.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
