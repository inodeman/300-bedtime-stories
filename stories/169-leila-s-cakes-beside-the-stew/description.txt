Leila's Cakes Beside the Stew is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Leila brings a basket of corn cakes to the harvest supper and sees Mrs. Nguyen's stew has bread that ran out. She splits her cakes so people can taste cake and stew together. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing food means putting different dishes where people can enjoy them together.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
