Micah and the Warm Extra Muffin is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Micah bakes a small tray of muffins and, when a neighbor child returns a bowl at the door, shares the warm one he wanted so they can eat together. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness can mean giving the warm piece you hoped to keep.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
