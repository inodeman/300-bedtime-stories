The Lemon Tin on the Slow Ferry | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Lemon Tin on the Slow Ferry is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Leandro holds a tin of lemon slices meant for everyone's warm mint tea on the slow ferry. He wants them all. When he sees there is enough for one slice each, he offers the tin around, and the shared sweetness makes the ride feel wider. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing a favorite thing can make a slow ride feel generous and sweet.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
