Slow on Purpose aboard Little Reed is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Keiko wants the canal boat to hurry, then sits with Grandpa and Ms. Fenn and draws the dragonfly, reeds, and lilies that only a slow loop reveals. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A slow ride can show more when you let the banks stay readable.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
