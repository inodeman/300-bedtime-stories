The Shady Corner Friend is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Amina's basil droops, and she feels guilty. Her friend Boris sees that the pot is sitting in harsh sun. Together they move it to a shady corner, water it gently, and promise to be noticing friends the next time something needs help. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A friend helps by noticing the problem and sharing the work.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
