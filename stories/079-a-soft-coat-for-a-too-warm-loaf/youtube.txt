A Soft Coat for a Too-Warm Loaf | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

A Soft Coat for a Too-Warm Loaf is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Giorgio's fresh loaf is too hot to hold, so Sable wraps it in an extra cloth and they walk it to the shared table with Emile. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A small loan of comfort can make a hot walk kinder.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
