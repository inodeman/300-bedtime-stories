Kenji Tells the Pot the Truth | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Kenji Tells the Pot the Truth is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Kenji is stirring soup when the pot starts bubbling faster than he was told it should, and instead of staying quiet he tells Grandma so they can lower the heat together. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage is speaking up early so a small worry can be fixed with care.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
