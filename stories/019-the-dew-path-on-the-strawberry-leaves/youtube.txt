The Dew Path on the Strawberry Leaves | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Dew Path on the Strawberry Leaves is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Ivy rushes to pick strawberries before breakfast and then pauses to watch the dew, a snail's silver path, and the way morning light changes the leaves. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Wonder is stopping long enough to notice what a morning is doing.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
