The Square Cushion on the Round Rug | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Square Cushion on the Round Rug is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Hanan's reading circle on the plaza has a round rug, but her big square cushion fills the middle so two friends cannot see the borrowed picture book. She moves the cushion to the edge and makes room. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Friendship sometimes means moving what you like so someone else can sit close.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
