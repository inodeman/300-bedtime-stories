Karim's Hello the Size of a Cinnamon Seed | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Karim's Hello the Size of a Cinnamon Seed is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Karim practices a small hello with Chioma and Pavel, then stands beside the bread and welcomes the neighbors even though his voice feels tiny. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can be a short, true welcome said beside the bread.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
