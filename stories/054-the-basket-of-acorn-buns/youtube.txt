The Basket of Acorn Buns | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Basket of Acorn Buns is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Callum shares a basket of warm acorn buns with foxes in the picnic clearing and leaves some at the library and the bridge. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing a plenty makes more places feel full.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
