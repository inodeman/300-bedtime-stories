The Little Skiff and the Slow Tide is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Nia wants to drag the little skiff down to the water at once, but the tide is still out, so she waits and watches until the sea comes up and lifts the boat gently. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience lets the right moment arrive without a struggle.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
