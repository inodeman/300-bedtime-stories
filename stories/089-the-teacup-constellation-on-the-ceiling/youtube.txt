The Teacup Constellation on the Ceiling | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Teacup Constellation on the Ceiling is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Goldie plans a glowing teacup on the ceiling, adds paper stars, and shares one sticker with Grandpa so the room holds a homemade constellation. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Wonder can be a sky you visit and a picture you make.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
