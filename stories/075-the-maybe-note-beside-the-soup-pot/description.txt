The Maybe Note Beside the Soup Pot is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Benji wonders why the recipe says orange peel might go in later, so he, Ayo, and Bea taste both ways and keep a cozy bowl and a bright bowl. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Curiosity can show that the same peel makes two different good soups.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
