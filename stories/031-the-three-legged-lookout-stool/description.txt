The Three Legged Lookout Stool is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Elodie finds the keeper's three-legged wooden stool wobbling on the cottage step and carries it up to the windy lookout so he can sit while he watches the boats. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness can be as simple as carrying the thing someone else needs for a rest.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
