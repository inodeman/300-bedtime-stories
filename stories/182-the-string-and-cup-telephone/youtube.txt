The String and Cup Telephone | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The String and Cup Telephone is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Dina wants to tell Ekon a bedtime joke, but her voice feels too small to call across the gap. They build a string-and-cup telephone. The first try fails because the string is slack. With Papa's help they tighten it, and Dina bravely sends her joke through the cup. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A quiet voice can still reach a friend when you try, and when the string is pulled tight.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
