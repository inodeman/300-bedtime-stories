The Labels That Lost Their Argument is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Amira smells and tastes the sample spoons, then tells Lewis and Otto that the amber and orange labels were swapped so they can fix them before neighbors choose. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Speaking up about a mix-up keeps the next person from getting a surprise they did not choose.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
