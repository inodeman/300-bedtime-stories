Hana and the Damp Cushion | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Hana and the Damp Cushion is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Hana spills a cup of water on the treehouse story cushion and chooses to tell her cousin and help dry it so everyone can keep reading. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness tells the truth about a mess and stays to help tidy it.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
