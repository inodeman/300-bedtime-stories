The Cricket Under the Mint Leaves is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Sylvain listens to a cricket in the mint, waters the pot slowly, and learns with Grandma that the night garden needs care more than owning. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A garden is for careful noticing, not for catching.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
