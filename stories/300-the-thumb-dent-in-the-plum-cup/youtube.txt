The Thumb Dent in the Plum Cup | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Thumb Dent in the Plum Cup is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Soren makes two cream cups with plum-purple rims for sharing juice at the studio window, but his thumb leaves a dent he hopes no one will see. He tells his friend the truth, and together they smooth the dent and shape the cup again. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Honest friends can tell the truth kindly and try a careful fix together.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
