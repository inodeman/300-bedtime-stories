Orhan's Questions in the Meadow | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Orhan's Questions in the Meadow is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Orhan walks the meadow with a notebook of gentle questions about the heron, the clover, the pony, and the rabbits. He watches from kind distances, learns some answers, and writes that he does not know yet for the rest. His curiosity stays respectful and wide awake. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Curiosity asks kind questions and is glad to learn, even when the answer is not ready yet.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
