Flora Watches the Bees from the Path | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Flora Watches the Bees from the Path is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Flora hears bees in the clover and wants to see their work. With a grown-up she stays on the path, draws what she notices, and learns that careful distance is part of being curious. She leaves the flowers for the bees and brings her drawing home. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Curiosity is wonderful when we look closely and still give wild neighbors plenty of room.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
