The First Step on the Painted Moon Stool is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Leila is nervous about stepping onto the stool to see the moon, then takes the first step with Mama and finds the rail is only a rail. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage often lives in the first small move.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
