The Pocket of Smooth Cove Stones | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Pocket of Smooth Cove Stones is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Hana has a pocket full of smooth stones for a seaside picture, and when her cousin Bram arrives with nothing to add, she shares the stones so they can build the picture together. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing what you collected can give someone else a way to join in.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
