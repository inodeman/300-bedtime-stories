The Picnic Basket Pulley | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Picnic Basket Pulley is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Basil and Cleo want to bring a picnic basket up to a low platform without squashing the sandwiches. With Nana's help they build a small pulley. The basket tilts on the first try, so they add a second loop, and the picnic rides up steady and straight. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: When people share the pulling and the planning, a heavy basket can rise smoothly.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
