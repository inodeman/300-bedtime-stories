Rosa and the Second Orchard Step | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Rosa and the Second Orchard Step is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Rosa wants to pick a ripe pear for Grandma's basket but hesitates at the short ladder, then climbs one step and the next while Grandma holds it steady. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can be one small step, and then one more, with a steady helper nearby.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
