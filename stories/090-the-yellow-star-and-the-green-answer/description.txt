The Yellow Star and the Green Answer is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Malik lifts a yellow paper star, his friend answers with a green one, and both houses share the same moon before a quiet bedtime. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Friendship can wave across a garden without a loud voice.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
