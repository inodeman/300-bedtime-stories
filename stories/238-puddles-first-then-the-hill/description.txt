Puddles First, Then the Hill is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Rina wants to follow the puddle pieces and Carmen wants the hill, so they do both walks and keep the rainbow wide enough for two wishes. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Two good wishes can both fit when friends choose the order together.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
