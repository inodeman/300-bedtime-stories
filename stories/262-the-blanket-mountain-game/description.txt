The Blanket Mountain Game is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Boris and Chiara's play leaves the bedroom full of socks, books, and blankets. They feel overwhelmed until they invent a tidy game with a map keeper, a guide, and gentle rules. Together they put every item home and leave a sign so the next playtime can begin kindly. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Teamwork can turn a big job into a kind game.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
