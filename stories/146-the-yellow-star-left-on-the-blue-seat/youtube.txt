The Yellow Star Left on the Blue Seat | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Yellow Star Left on the Blue Seat is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Idris finds a paper star pinned to a ticket stub, wants it, and gives it to the driver so it can return to Lale. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Wanting a pretty thing is not the same as keeping it.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
