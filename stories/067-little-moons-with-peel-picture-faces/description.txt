Little Moons with Peel-Picture Faces is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Benji, Ayo, and Bea decorate matching rolls with orange-peel pictures, warming the peel until the shapes stay whole for the stall. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A careful pause can turn a plain roll into a picture worth sharing.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
