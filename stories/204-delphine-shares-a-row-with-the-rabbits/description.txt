Delphine Shares a Row with the Rabbits is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Delphine wants lettuce for a salad, but rabbits also nibble the garden border. She marks one clover row to share and one row to keep. Sitting quietly, she learns that sharing can be generous and still be fair. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing works best when there is a clear, kind plan so everyone has enough.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
