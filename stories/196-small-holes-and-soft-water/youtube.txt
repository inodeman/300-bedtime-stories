Small Holes and Soft Water | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Small Holes and Soft Water is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Soren and Tova water a neighbor's seedlings while she is at the library. With a grown-up they make a bottle sprinkler. Too many holes make the water gush, so they tape some shut until the shower is soft. The soil darkens, the leaves smell green, and the seedlings are ready for their neighbor's return. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Nature thrives with gentle care, and a kind tool can be made softer when the first try is too strong.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
