The Second Leaf Whistle is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Mateo makes a second leaf-roll whistle and shares three notes with a young fox who has been listening from the ferns. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Friendship grows when you leave room and a place for another voice.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
