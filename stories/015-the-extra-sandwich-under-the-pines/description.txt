The Extra Sandwich under the Pines is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Kit packs two sandwiches by habit and, when Jules forgets lunch on their woods walk, shares the extra so both friends can eat together on the picnic stone. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing your extra makes room for a friend at the table.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
