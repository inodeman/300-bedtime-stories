Stitches That Took Their Time | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Stitches That Took Their Time is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Dario and Esme sew a pillow for the hard window seat. A knot is loosened, a puckered corner is unpicked, and they rest every ten stitches. The finished yellow pillow gives elbows a soft place in the sun. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience is pausing, unpicking, and trying a corner again with shorter steps.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
