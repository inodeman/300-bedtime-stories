The Apple Fan That Would Not Close | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Apple Fan That Would Not Close is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Felix rides to the common with too many apple slices, then shares a fan of three with Pim and learns a kite-tail knot in return. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A basket packed a little fuller than one person can become a kindness with wheels.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
