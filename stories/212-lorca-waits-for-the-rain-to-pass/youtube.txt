Lorca Waits for the Rain to Pass | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Lorca Waits for the Rain to Pass is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Lorca and Corin have a light picnic ready for Moss to carry when a soft rain begins. Lorca wants to go at once. They wait on the porch until the path is kinder. After the rain they enjoy a brighter meadow and a donkey with surer footing. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience can turn a delay into a gentler, happier plan.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
