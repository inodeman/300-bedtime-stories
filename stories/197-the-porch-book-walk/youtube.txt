The Porch Book Walk | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Porch Book Walk is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Wynn and Jamila set out to return borrowed books, but the books go toward the wrong porches. They notice, tell each other, and make simple name cards. Then they deliver each book to the right friend, including Ivo, and read one page together on his step. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Friendship grows when we walk together, fix a mix-up, and save a page to share.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
