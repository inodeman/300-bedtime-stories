Tariq and the Mixed-Up Notes is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Tariq swaps notes meant for Omar and Ruth. He says so at once, they trade the notes back, and they add small pictures so the next delivery is easier to get right. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Friends help a kind idea find the right person, even after a mix-up.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
