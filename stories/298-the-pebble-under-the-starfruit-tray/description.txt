The Pebble Under the Starfruit Tray is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

A stack of cane starfruit trays leans because a pebble sits under one foot. Puck steadies the stack but cannot sweep the pebble alone. With the fruit seller and a lamp helper, they lift a little, sweep, and set the tray flat together. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Teamwork is each person doing one small safe part so the whole job holds.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
