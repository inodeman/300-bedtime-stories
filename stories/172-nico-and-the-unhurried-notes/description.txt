Nico and the Unhurried Notes is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Nico practices a simple recorder tune for the town music corner, but the notes tangle when he hurries. He slows his fingers, plays only the ready phrase, and joins the soft music without rushing. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience turns a jumble of notes into a tune that people can follow.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
