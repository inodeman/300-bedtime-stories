Mei Follows the Paper Leaf is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Mei finds a hand-drawn library map in a picture book. She and Omar follow it to nature books and a seed jar, then add kind new questions for the next readers. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Curiosity is careful looking that leaves a new question for the next friend.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
