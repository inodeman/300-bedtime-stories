Nora and the Stiff Treehouse Latch | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Nora and the Stiff Treehouse Latch is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Nora wants to join the afternoon story in the oak treehouse, but the latch is stiff and she feels shy about asking, so she climbs the last steps and asks for help, and together they open the door. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can be a small voice that asks for help at the right moment.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
