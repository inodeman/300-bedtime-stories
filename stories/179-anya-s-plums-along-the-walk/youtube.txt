Anya's Plums Along the Walk | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Anya's Plums Along the Walk is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Anya brings plums from the backyard tree on the town lantern walk. She offers them one by one to neighbors and musicians, saving a few to share at home. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing tastes sweeter when there is enough left for the next person too.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
