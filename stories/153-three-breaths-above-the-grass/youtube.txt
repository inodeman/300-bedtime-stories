Three Breaths Above the Grass | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Three Breaths Above the Grass is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Pascal is nervous about the low tethered balloon, then climbs in, counts with Auntie, and names the post box, the white tent, and a green shoe. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can be sitting in the basket and counting with someone close.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
