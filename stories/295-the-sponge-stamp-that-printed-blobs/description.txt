The Sponge Stamp That Printed Blobs is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Mireille's seed-shaped sponge stamp prints blobs on the mural's orchard corner because it is too wet. She tries again by blotting, pressing lightly, and lifting straight, until a row of clear seeds appears. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Trying a lighter touch can turn a blob into the shape you hoped for.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
