The Puzzle Petal Under the Lily Leaves | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Puzzle Petal Under the Lily Leaves is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

One wooden petal of the fountain's floating lily puzzle has drifted under the real leaves. Ruya wants to stir the water quickly. The fountain minder shows her how to wait and watch the slow turn of the water until the petal comes around to her hands. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience lets the water bring back what a rush would push farther away.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
