Ada and the Second Hello | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Ada and the Second Hello is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Ada wants to leave kind notes, but paint on her thumb smears the first one. Mei helps her wash up and write sunny notes for friends, including a spare hello so no mailbox feels left out. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A smudge is not the end of a kind idea, and trying again can fill an empty mailbox with warmth.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
