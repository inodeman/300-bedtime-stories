The Spare Acorn Cap Hat is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Joost lines a large acorn cap with moss to make a spare hat and keeps it ready for the fox friend who visits when the fireflies wake. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Friendship remembers a small comfort and sets it out before anyone must ask.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
