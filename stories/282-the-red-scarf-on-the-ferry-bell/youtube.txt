The Red Scarf on the Ferry Bell | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Red Scarf on the Ferry Bell is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Zayd rides the slow ferry with a grown-up and notices the red signal scarf has slipped from the kind bell, so walkers on the bridge cannot see the boat coming. He helps the ferry guide retie it where it can be seen. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Noticing a small loose thing and helping to fix it lets other people feel included.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
