Cards on the Kindness Line is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Ivo wants to thank a neighbor for lemons but feels shy. With Basil he hangs painted cards on a short line. The wind takes one card, so they add a second pin. Ivo finds the courage to clip his own thank-you where it can be read, and the neighbor answers with a wave. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can be a small card clipped where a neighbor will see your true thanks.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
