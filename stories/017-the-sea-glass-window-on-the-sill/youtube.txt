The Sea-Glass Window on the Sill | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Sea-Glass Window on the Sill is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Leo finds a piece of sea glass and wonders about its long gentle journey, then places it on the sill where light can show its color to anyone who pauses. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Wonder turns a small find into a story you can share.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
