The Fern Height Stick is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Paolo checks a fern-frond height stick all day until a slow fern reaches the shade mark without being rushed. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience lets you notice good things that grow in their own time.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
