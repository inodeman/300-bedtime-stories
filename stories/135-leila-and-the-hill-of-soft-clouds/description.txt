Leila and the Hill of Soft Clouds is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Leila hopes to point out the far town from the hill, but a soft mist hides it, so she watches cloud shapes and listens for the town bell instead. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Wonder can turn a changed plan into a sky full of new things to see.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
