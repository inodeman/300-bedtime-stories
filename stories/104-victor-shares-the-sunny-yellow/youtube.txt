Victor Shares the Sunny Yellow | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Victor Shares the Sunny Yellow is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Victor has the last full cup of yellow paint, and Salma needs a little for her poster sun. They share the cup and mix a honey-gold for the edges so both fair posters glow. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing a color can make two pictures brighter than keeping it all.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
