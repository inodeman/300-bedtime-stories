The Towel on the Library Steps is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Nia thinks the picnic is ruined, so Carmen shares a dry towel, waits with her, and they name the rainbow together. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness can sit quietly until a missed sunny hour becomes a rainbow.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
