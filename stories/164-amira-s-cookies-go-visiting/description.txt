Amira's Cookies Go Visiting is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Amira brings date cookies to the festival sharing table and notices the sweets are all at one end. She carries half the remaining cookies to the far side so everyone gets a turn. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing means making sure the sweetness reaches people at the far end too.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
