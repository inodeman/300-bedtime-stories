Kira Brings Shade and Fresh Water | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Kira Brings Shade and Fresh Water is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

On a still warm afternoon Kira notices the chickens and Bramble resting in thin shade. With the grown-up she sets out fresh water and hangs a sheet the animals can walk under or around. She learns that kindness is checking on friends and leaving them free. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness can be as simple as noticing what a warm day needs and offering comfort.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
