The Soft Spare Rope is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Sylvie carries a coil of soft spare rope onto the rope footbridge and adds a guide line beside a loose loop. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can be a steady step and a careful knot.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
