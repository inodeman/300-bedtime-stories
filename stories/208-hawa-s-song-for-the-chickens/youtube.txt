Hawa's Song for the Chickens | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Hawa's Song for the Chickens is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Hawa wants the chickens' breakfast to feel like a tiny celebration. She invents a gentle calling song and decorates the outside of their oat bowls with dry, simple patterns. The chickens come because they wish to, and Hawa learns that making something kind is a creative act. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Creativity can turn an ordinary kindness into something joyful and new.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
