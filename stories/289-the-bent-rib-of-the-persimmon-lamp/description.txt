The Bent Rib of the Persimmon Lamp is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At the early evening fruit market, Gio sees a persimmon-shaped paper lamp with a bent wire rib, so the light hides the pale fruit slices. He wants to yank the wire. The lamp maker shows him how to warm his hands and ease the rib back a little at a time. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A gentle, patient hand can straighten what a quick pull might crumple.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
