The Coin Puddle and the Soil Pantry is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Oleg and Bjorn watch a stone puddle shrink to a coin while the soil keeps a cool pantry, then mulch a crack so roots can use the drink. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Water teaches two lessons when one puddle vanishes and another stays cool.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
