The Barley Pillow Waiting by the Lamp | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Barley Pillow Waiting by the Lamp is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

While Grandpa names stars outside, Esther warms his barley pillow, turns his slippers toward the door, and pours his milk so the room feels looked after. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness can finish its quiet work before anyone has to ask.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
