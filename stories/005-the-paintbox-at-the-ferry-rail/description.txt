The Paintbox at the Ferry Rail is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Pim thinks her painting of the harbor looks wrong until she watches the water long enough to see its colors change, then paints what she really sees. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Wonder grows when you look long enough to see a thing change.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
