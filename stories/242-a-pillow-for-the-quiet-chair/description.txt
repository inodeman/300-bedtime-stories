A Pillow for the Quiet Chair is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Celine and Dario sew a pillow so Auntie's reading chair will feel gentler. Uneven stitches get eased, stuffing is spread so no lump hides, and Auntie stands by for the last snip. They give the pillow without a boast, and it does its quiet job. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A kindness you can lean on is made of careful stitches and shared work.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
