Ticket Stubs That Learned to Ride is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Vito tips out old ticket stubs and pastes them into memory pictures of the train, the bus, and the tethered balloon, leaving one blank scrap for a ride he has not taken. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Scraps can become a true picture when you give them a place in the story.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
