Maude Speaks Up for a Lighter Load is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Rafi, excited for a walk, stacks extra jars into Moss's basket and forgets the light-picnic rule. Maude feels unsure because she is younger, then speaks up kindly. The jars come out, Moss carries only a light load, and everyone thanks Maude for her clear voice. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can sound like a calm voice that protects a friend.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
