The Leaf Sail That Spun in Circles is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Eero's cork-and-leaf boat spins in circles in the plaza fountain because the leaf sail is too wide. He folds the sail smaller and adds a light pebble weight so the boat can travel calmly to the smiling stone turtle. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Changing one small part can help a whole idea work.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
