The Stamp That Faced the Other Way is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Esme and Fabio print leaves, stars, and dots. A letter comes out backward, so they test until they see that a stamp shows its other face. A sleeve smudge becomes a cloud, and they hang a finished banner that tells what they learned. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Curiosity asks what happened, then tries again to learn the answer.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
