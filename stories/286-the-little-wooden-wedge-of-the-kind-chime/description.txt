The Little Wooden Wedge of the Kind Chime is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Dalia hears that the clock tower's kind chime sounds stuffed and sleepy. With a grown-up she visits the bright clock room and finds the tiny wooden wedge, which keeps the hammer resting kindly, lying on the stair. They put it back so the chime rings clear again. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Noticing a change in a familiar sound, and telling someone kindly, helps the whole plaza.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
