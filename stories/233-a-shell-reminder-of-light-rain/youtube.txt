A Shell Reminder of Light Rain | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

A Shell Reminder of Light Rain is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Miren and Elif test shells, beads, and a spoon until their wind chime sounds like light rain with rests, then leave it uncrowded. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Making can stop when the sound is close enough and the rests are still there.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
