Emeka Stands Still like the Heron | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Emeka Stands Still like the Heron is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Emeka wants the patient heron to move and play. He learns to stand quietly instead. By waiting, he sees leaf boats, ripples, and a dragonfly, and the heron offers a calm turn of the head. Emeka discovers that patience is a way of being a good guest. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience lets you notice small wonders you would miss if you hurried.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
