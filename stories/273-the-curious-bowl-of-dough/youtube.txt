The Curious Bowl of Dough | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Curious Bowl of Dough is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Finn wants to know why a quiet bowl of dough grows bigger. Grandma Elena explains the tiny yeast helpers and lets him draw the before and the after. He waits for the timer, peeks once with care, and finds a soft pillow full of hidden bubbles. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Curiosity asks, waits, and looks carefully again.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
