Selma Leaves the Flowers for the Bees | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Selma Leaves the Flowers for the Bees is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Selma wants a large bouquet for the table. Watching bees from the path, she sees that the flowers are their pantry. She picks none of the busy blooms, makes a paper bouquet instead, and learns that loving a meadow can mean leaving it as it is. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Caring for nature means leaving enough of the meadow for the creatures who live there.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
