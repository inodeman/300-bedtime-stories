The Tin of Honey Shortbread is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Theo means to eat a whole tin of honey shortbread alone on the dunes, then opens it for Auntie and the lighthouse keeper, who climb up from the boats with empty hands. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing a favorite treat can make the taste feel bigger, not smaller.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
