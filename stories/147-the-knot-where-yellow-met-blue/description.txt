The Knot Where Yellow Met Blue is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Jo's pear kite and Ren's fish kite cross in the air, so they hold the winders still and loosen the knot one loop at a time. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A tangle comes undone when nobody yanks and everybody has a job.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
