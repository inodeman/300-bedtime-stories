Honey on One Half, Orange on the Other is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Otto wants honey and Reina wants orange peel, so with Hugo they leave a pale stripe and share both halves of one bake. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: One loaf can hold two favorites when friends leave a meeting place in the middle.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
