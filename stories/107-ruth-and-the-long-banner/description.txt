Ruth and the Long Banner is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

The fair banner is too long for one child to hold and paint. Ruth, Pietro, and Faye trade jobs, add cloth when the word needs room, and hang a straight, friendly welcome. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Teamwork is each person holding a different part of the same welcome.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
