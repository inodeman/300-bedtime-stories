Windows That Wanted to Open is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Piotr and Queisha explore a cardboard castle. They learn why it echoes, ask Papa to make a hinge hole while they hold the edges, and discover a loose thread loop that lets a flag spin. They draw the answers on the inside wall. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Curiosity tests one question at a time until the answer fits in your hands.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
