Boats That Carried Kind Words | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Boats That Carried Kind Words is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Hester and Imani fold paper boats and float short kind notes across a basin. A soggy boat retires as a note holder, and a new shared boat carries two messages at once. They hang the dry notes where they can read them again. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Friendship grows when kind words are made, sent, and kept.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
