The Sunflower Measuring Stick is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Oscar and Paz want to see how their sunflower grows. They mark a stick, but an evening shadow tricks them into thinking the plant shrank. The next morning they measure the stem itself with a level string and Grandpa's help, and they notice bees, scent, and rough leaves along the way. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Nature shows us more when we look at the living plant and not only at its shadow.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
