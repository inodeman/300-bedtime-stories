The Little House for Small Wings is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Kofi and Laila paint a birdhouse from wood Uncle already cut and smoothed. They hold the pieces while Uncle fits them, choose a shady middle-height spot, and set out seed and water. The finished house waits calmly for birds. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Teamwork means each person does a needed job, including the quiet ones.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
