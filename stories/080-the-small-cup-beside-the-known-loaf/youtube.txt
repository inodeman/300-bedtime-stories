The Small Cup Beside the Known Loaf | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Small Cup Beside the Known Loaf is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Laleh feels shy about a new orange-cinnamon soup beside the familiar loaves until she offers a small cup and a true welcome. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can begin with one true sentence and a sample you are willing to share.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
