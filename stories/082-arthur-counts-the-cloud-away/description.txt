Arthur Counts the Cloud Away is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Arthur wants the moon at once, but a thin cloud is in the way, so he counts cricket songs with Grandma and looks again until the sky is clear. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience lets your eyes visit twice instead of rushing a blur.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
