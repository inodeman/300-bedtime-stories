The Uninked Map Corner is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

June follows the blank corner of her paper map a short safe way and draws the sunny fern pocket she finds. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Curiosity can turn a blank space into a true and friendly place.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
