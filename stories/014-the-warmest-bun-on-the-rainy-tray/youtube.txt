The Warmest Bun on the Rainy Tray | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Warmest Bun on the Rainy Tray is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

On a rainy day Bea saves the warmest bun from the tray for Mr. Sato, the mail carrier, instead of keeping it for herself. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness is saving the best warmth for the person still out in the weather.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
