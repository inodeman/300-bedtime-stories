A Small Hello at the Big Table is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Una feels shy at a crowded family table and takes the chair beside the bread. Cousin Tomas asks a gentle question, and Una manages a small yes, passes the basket, and mentions her new mint leaf. Her shyness stays, and her courage sits right beside it. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can be one true sentence and one kind action.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
