Lila and the Shared Handle | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Lila and the Shared Handle is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Lila notices Grandma Nia's basket of dumplings is heavy on the way to the harvest table. She shares the handle, matches Grandma's slow steps, and sets the food where every hand can reach. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness can be as simple as matching someone's pace and sharing the weight.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
