Crackers for a Short Ride is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Quinn opens a full tin of crackers for Noor, Esti, and their father after a bag of rolls tips, and the short ride home becomes a shared table. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing a tin can turn a tipped bag into a conversation.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
