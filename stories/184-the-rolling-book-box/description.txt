The Rolling Book Box is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Helena and Ivo build a homemade book cart from a shallow crate, cardboard sides, and spool wheels so they can share picture books with Jamila. On the first roll, a book slides off. They add higher sides and a soft strap, then deliver the books with a smile. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing stories is easier when we carry them carefully and leave enough for everyone.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
