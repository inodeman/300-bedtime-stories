Three Bracelets and a Good Morning is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Jules and Kofi knot three bracelets, one for each other and one for the neighbor who brings good mornings with the letters. They redo a gap so the gift is comfortable, deliver it kindly, and keep two bracelets for their own wrists. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness notices someone's ordinary help and says thank you with your hands.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
