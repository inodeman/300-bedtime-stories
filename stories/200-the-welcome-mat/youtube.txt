The Welcome Mat | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Welcome Mat is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Ekon feels shy about greeting Dina, who has just moved into the calm house next door. He and Papa paint a simple cardboard welcome mat, and Ekon brings a labeled jar of crackers. His first hello is too quiet to hear. He tries again, Dina answers, and they share the crackers on the step. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can sound like a quiet hello when you try again and bring a small kindness with you.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
