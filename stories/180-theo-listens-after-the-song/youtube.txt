Theo Listens After the Song | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Theo Listens After the Song is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

When the festival's quiet music ends, Theo stays a little longer and listens to spoons, crickets, paper lanterns, and one last flute breath become a new kind of song. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Wonder stays when you listen to the quiet that follows the music.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
