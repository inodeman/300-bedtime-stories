One Picture Then the Story is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Pema wants to flip the album quickly to find the best picture. Grandpa Kamal asks her to stay with one rainy indoor picnic and hold the ribbon until the story ends. She hears about spoon tent poles and a slow tomato, then saves the rest of the pages for next time. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience lets a story finish before the next page turns.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
