The Braid Longer Than Pavel's Arms is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Pavel's honey braid will not fit one basket, so he, Emile, and Nkechi rest it, loosen it into three pieces, and send the bread to more than one table. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A gift too big for one basket is just right when it is shared.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
