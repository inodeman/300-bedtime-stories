Alba and the Goat Who Wanted a Hat | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Alba and the Goat Who Wanted a Hat is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Alba sees that Bramble the goat is curious about sun hats. With the grown-up, she sews a light loose hat the goat can wear or shake off. They walk the meadow together, and Alba learns that kindness means helping a wish without ever making a friend feel stuck. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness notices a friend's wish and answers it in a way that stays gentle and free.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
