Salma and the Two Jars | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Salma and the Two Jars is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Salma wonders why one seed jar is taller. She and Kwame see that books had shaded the shorter jar. They move the shade, leave a seed coat alone, and write an honest log. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Curiosity looks closely, tells the truth, and lets living things answer in their own time.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
