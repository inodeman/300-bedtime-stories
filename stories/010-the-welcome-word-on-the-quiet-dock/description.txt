The Welcome Word on the Quiet Dock is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Mateo is asked to be the one who says the ferry welcome to the first passengers, and he practices until his voice comes out clear and kind. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can be using your voice to make someone feel expected.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
