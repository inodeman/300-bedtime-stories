A Perch Beside the Mint | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

A Perch Beside the Mint is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Laila and Marco pause their painting to watch a small bird. They help Auntie place the finished birdhouse at middle height near the mint, with a clean perch, seed, and water. The bird samples a seed, and they keep listening. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Nature shows you where a made thing belongs if you listen and watch.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
