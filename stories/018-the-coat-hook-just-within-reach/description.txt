The Coat Hook Just Within Reach is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Sora sees that Nico's coat keeps missing the high hook, so she shows him a lower hook and how the loop works, staying beside him until the coat hangs safely. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness is helping someone reach what they need without taking over.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
