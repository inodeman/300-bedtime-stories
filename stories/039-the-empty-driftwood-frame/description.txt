The Empty Driftwood Frame is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Rufus sorts sea glass and empty shells into a plain driftwood frame so the lighthouse cottage wall can hold a homemade picture of the cove. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Creativity can fill an empty edge with small careful wonders from a day outside.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
