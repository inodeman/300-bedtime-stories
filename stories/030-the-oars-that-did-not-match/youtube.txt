The Oars That Did Not Match | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Oars That Did Not Match is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Samir and the lighthouse keeper row their loop with two wooden oars that do not match, and they laugh, rebalance, and finish the friendship circuit side by side. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Friendship stays steady when you adjust together and keep the promise to show up.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
