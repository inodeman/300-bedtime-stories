Samir's Ribbon at the Parcel Table is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Samir brings a long ribbon to tie a parcel at the post office, meets a child whose string has frayed, and shares the ribbon so both parcels can be mailed. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing what you have enough of can help two good things go out into the world.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
