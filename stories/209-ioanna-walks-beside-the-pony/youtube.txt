Ioanna Walks Beside the Pony | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Ioanna Walks Beside the Pony is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Ioanna feels unsure about walking beside Pippin the pony for the first time. A grown-up stays with them the whole way. They go only as far as the first oak and back. Ioanna learns that courage can be quiet, short, and completely kind. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can be a small new step taken with a trusted grown-up close by.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
