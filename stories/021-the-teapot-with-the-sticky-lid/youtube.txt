The Teapot with the Sticky Lid | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Teapot with the Sticky Lid is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

When the salt-crusted lid of Mira's enamel teapot sticks shut on the dune picnic, she works it open and pours the first cup for the chilly lighthouse keeper. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness is noticing who needs warmth and offering it first.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
