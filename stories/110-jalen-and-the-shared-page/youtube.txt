Jalen and the Shared Page | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Jalen and the Shared Page is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Jalen finds Polly reading the boat book he hoped to open. He sits beside her, and they notice different details together, then leave a kind note for the next readers. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Friendship grows when a saved seat becomes a shared story.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
