The Tide Chart under the Shell is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Ellis wants to launch his green-striped wooden boat at once, but the shell-pinned tide chart says to wait, and his patience lets the rising water lift the hull instead of scraping it. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Waiting for the right moment can protect the fun you hope to have.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
