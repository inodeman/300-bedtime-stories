The Cinnamon Buns That Held Hands is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Bea, Hugo, and Tessa cover the stuck cinnamon buns with a warm cloth and wait until the spiral loosens into whole pieces for the shared table. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience lets a stuck thing loosen without being torn.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
