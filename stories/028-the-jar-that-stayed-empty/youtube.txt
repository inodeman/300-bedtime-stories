The Jar That Stayed Empty | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Jar That Stayed Empty is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Leif wants to take a tiny shore crab home in his glass jar, then watches it live safely in the tide pool and pours every drop of seawater back. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Loving a wild animal means letting it keep the home that knows how to hold it.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
