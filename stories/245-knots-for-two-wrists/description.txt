Knots for Two Wrists is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Imani and Jules knot friendship bracelets for each other, using favorite colors. Lumpy knots are undone and a tangle is opened slowly. They trade jobs so both hands are in each gift, and they wear the finished bracelets without pinching or bragging. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Friendship can be a row of gentle knots that leave room to be yourself.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
