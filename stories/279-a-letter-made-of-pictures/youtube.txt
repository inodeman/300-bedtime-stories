A Letter Made of Pictures | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

A Letter Made of Pictures is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Habiba misses cousin Lotta and feels stuck after the first word of a letter. With Grandpa Kamal's encouragement she draws the kitchen, a saved stool, a cloud pancake, mint, and a path between towns. Sending the pictures makes the missing feel seen and gently connected. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Creativity can carry kind news when words feel hard.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
