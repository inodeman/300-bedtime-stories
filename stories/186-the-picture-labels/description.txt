The Picture Labels is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Luna moves the kitchen jars while dusting and then feels worried. Malik almost spreads spicy pickle on bread. Luna tells the truth. Together, with Mama's help, they taste carefully and make picture labels so every jar tells the truth on the outside. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Telling the truth about a mix-up helps everyone find the right jar.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
