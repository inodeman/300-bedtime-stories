The Cheek Test for a Sleepy Kite | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Cheek Test for a Sleepy Kite is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Orla's kite will not rise in the still morning until she waits, feels the wind on her cheek, and lets go only when the ribbon of air is real. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience is doing the right small things until the moment arrives.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
