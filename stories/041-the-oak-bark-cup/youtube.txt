The Oak Bark Cup | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Oak Bark Cup is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Wren mends a chipped oak-bark cup so a gentle fox can share warm berry tea at her mushroom cottage. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A small kindness can mend a cup and make a friend feel welcome.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
