The Yellow Rope on the Dock is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Farah's stomach flutters on the wooden dock, but she reaches for the yellow painter rope and ties the little wooden boat so it can rest snug and safe. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can be a careful step you take while a kind person stands close.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
