The Potato Boat in a Slow Cloud is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Davin thinks the clouds are plain until he and Paloma wait, then keep both a potato boat and a whale-mitten on their chalk board. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A slow cloud can hold two pictures if nobody has to win.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
