The Telescope That Needed Two Hands | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Telescope That Needed Two Hands is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Rohan and Auntie tape a paper ring onto a rolling tube together, then trade looks so the moon and the Big Dipper stay still. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Some round things stay useful only when two people share the job.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
