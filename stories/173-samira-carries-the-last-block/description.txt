Samira Carries the Last Block is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Samira asks to carry the family lantern for the short last block of the town walk. With Papa beside her, she keeps the light steady and feels proud of the careful new job. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can be trying a new task while someone who trusts you walks beside you.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
