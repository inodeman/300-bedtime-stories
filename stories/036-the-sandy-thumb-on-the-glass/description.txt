The Sandy Thumb on the Glass is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Cleo leaves a sandy thumbprint on the lantern glass while polishing and tells the keeper at once instead of turning the smear toward the curtain. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Honesty shines clearer than a hidden smudge ever could.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
