The Cup Naomi Warmed Again | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Cup Naomi Warmed Again is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Naomi warms Papa's forgotten milk, smooths a bent paper star, and looks with him through a cardboard tube at the moon and the Big Dipper. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kind help can be ready before anyone has to ask.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
