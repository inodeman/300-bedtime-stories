Elias Saves the Soft Place is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Elias saves the end of a bench with a blanket so Mr. Ade can sit with his drum. He also brings him water and keeps the rhythm soft instead of showing off. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness is keeping a place ready for someone who needs to rest.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
