Ursula's Round of Gentle Visits is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Ursula thinks she must bring a big gift to be a good friend. Walking from the dog to the goat, the heron, the bees, the chickens, and the pony, she offers each the greeting they already enjoy. She learns that a careful visit can be the whole present. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Friendship is remembering how each friend likes to be greeted and showing up with care.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
