The Thick Corner Big Enough for More | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Thick Corner Big Enough for More is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Cosmo gives Uncle the favorite thick corner, splits the star biscuits, and keeps the cardboard telescope in the middle for turns. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: The best spot grows kinder when more than one person can sit there.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
