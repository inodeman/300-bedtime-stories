Two Songs in the Sun, One Rest in the Shade | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Two Songs in the Sun, One Rest in the Shade is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Gracie brings an extra sun hat for squinting Tamsin, and they share two songs in the sun and one rest in the shade. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: An extra hat and a shared rest can turn a hot playground into a fair game.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
