The Oak That Moved to the True Side | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Oak That Moved to the True Side is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Sami almost hides that his oak circle is on the wrong side, then says so, and he and Papa follow the true path and darken the corrected map. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Naming a wobble while it is still small keeps a walk on the true path.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
