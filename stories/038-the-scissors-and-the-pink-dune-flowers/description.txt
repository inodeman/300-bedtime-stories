The Scissors and the Pink Dune Flowers is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Amina brings round-tipped scissors to cut pink dune flowers for the cottage table, sees the bees feeding, and draws the blossoms instead so they stay blooming. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Nature lets us visit its color without asking us to carry the color away.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
