The Pillow Line is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Omar and Tomas both want the window side of the bed where the morning light lands. They feel tight, then build a pillow line, trade nights, and open the curtain together. Each side becomes a good side once the sunrise is shared instead of grabbed. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing can turn one favorite spot into fair, friendly turns.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
