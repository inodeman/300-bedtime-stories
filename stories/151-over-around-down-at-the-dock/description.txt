Over, Around, Down at the Dock is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Nabil brings water to the dock, helps Ms. Fenn coil a slippery rope, and reprints the smeared chalkboard so visitors can read the rules. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A helper leaves a place easier than they found it.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
