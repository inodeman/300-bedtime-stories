Hugo and the Ferry Cracker Tin | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Hugo and the Ferry Cracker Tin is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

On an evening ferry ride Hugo's neighbor child spills their crackers into a napkin mess, and Hugo shares his own tin so both of them have a snack while they watch the harbor lights. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing a small snack can make a long ride feel friendly for everyone.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
