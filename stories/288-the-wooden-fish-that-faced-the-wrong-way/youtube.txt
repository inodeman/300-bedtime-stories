The Wooden Fish That Faced the Wrong Way | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Wooden Fish That Faced the Wrong Way is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Three painted wooden fish that show the safe walking side of the bridge have turned backward in a breeze. Farah cannot reach the cord alone. She works with the bridge mender and a small neighbor to turn the fish the right way. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Teamwork turns mixed-up markers back into a clear, welcoming path.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
