The Red Mitten on the Mossy Bench | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Red Mitten on the Mossy Bench is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Sam walks the short woods path by himself for the first time to return Ada's red mitten to the mossy bench where she will find it before the evening cools. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can be a steady walk to do a helpful thing.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
