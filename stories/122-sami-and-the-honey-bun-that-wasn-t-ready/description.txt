Sami and the Honey Bun That Wasn't Ready is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Sami wants a honey bun that is still baking at the night market, learns to wait instead of taking a rushed cold one, and helps at the stall until the bun is ready to share. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience lets good things finish becoming what they are meant to be.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
