The Leaf Stamp That Found Its Door | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Leaf Stamp That Found Its Door is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Rosa asks how mail finds her square door, writes a note to herself, and walks it from cubby to slot with Mr. Pell's gentle map of town, street, and number. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A letter finds a house because careful people point it.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
