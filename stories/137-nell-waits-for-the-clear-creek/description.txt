Nell Waits for the Clear Creek is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

After rain the creek is cloudy and Nell cannot see the stones she painted yesterday, so she waits on the bank until the water clears. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience lets muddy water settle until what you hope to see can appear.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
