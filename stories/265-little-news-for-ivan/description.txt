Little News for Ivan is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Finn misses cousin Ivan, who lives in another town, and stares at a blank page. Grandma Noelle helps him send small kitchen news, a drawing of Ivan's chair, and a humming spot by the sink. Mailing the letter turns the missing feeling into a kindness on its way. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kind, true news can warm someone who lives far away.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
