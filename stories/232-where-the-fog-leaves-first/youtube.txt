Where the Fog Leaves First | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Where the Fog Leaves First is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Luca and Haru study one mast, the bell, and the crates, and write that the friendly fog clears from the top and in patches. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Curiosity can watch a fog give the harbor back without rushing the white air.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
