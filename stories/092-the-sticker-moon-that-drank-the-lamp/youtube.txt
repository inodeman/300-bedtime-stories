The Sticker Moon That Drank the Lamp | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Sticker Moon That Drank the Lamp is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Nils wants a glow sticker to shine at once, then waits with Auntie while it drinks lamplight and wakes pale green in a dim corner. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Some brightness needs a quiet wait before it can shine back.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
