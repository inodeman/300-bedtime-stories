The Last Cinnamon Spiral | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Last Cinnamon Spiral is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

When only one cinnamon spiral is left in the case, Theo chooses to split it with Jonah, a new boy who missed breakfast, and both of them leave with enough sweetness. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing a small treat can make two people feel full.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
