Four Kind Beats at the Tangled Stall is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Labels, peels, and the tablecloth all arrive at once, so Hana, Giorgio, and Laleh count a shared beat for each job until the stall stands ready. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Naming turns keeps a busy stall from tangling every job at once.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
