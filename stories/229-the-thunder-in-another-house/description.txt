The Thunder in Another House is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Inez hears faraway thunder, stays inside with Dad and Jovan, and learns to count, draw, and say how she feels instead of peeking out. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Staying inside and telling the truth about a jumpy feeling is a brave, safe choice.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
