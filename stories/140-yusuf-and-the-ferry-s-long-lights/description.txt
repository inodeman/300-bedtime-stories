Yusuf and the Ferry's Long Lights is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Yusuf thinks the evening ferry ride is too long and empty, then notices reflections, rope smells, and stretched lights, and fills his postcard sketch with them. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Wonder fills a quiet ride when you let your eyes gather what is already there.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
