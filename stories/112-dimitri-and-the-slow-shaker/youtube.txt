Dimitri and the Slow Shaker | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Dimitri and the Slow Shaker is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Dimitri's fast shaking covers Hugh's hummed song. Hugh shows a shake-and-rest pattern, and Dimitri learns to pause so their shakers sound like gentle rain. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience gives a song its shape by leaving room between the sounds.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
