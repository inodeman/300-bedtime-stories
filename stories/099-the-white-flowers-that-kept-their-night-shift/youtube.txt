The White Flowers That Kept Their Night Shift | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The White Flowers That Kept Their Night Shift is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Mabel almost picks a perfect white balcony flower, then sniffs, draws, and waters just enough so the plant keeps its night choir. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Caring can mean leaving a flower where it can keep blooming.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
