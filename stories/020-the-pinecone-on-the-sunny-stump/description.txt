The Pinecone on the Sunny Stump is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Pip wants the seeds inside a closed pinecone right away and learns to leave it on a sunny stump until warmth opens the scales. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience is giving warmth and time instead of prying.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
