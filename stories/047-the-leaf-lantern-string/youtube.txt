The Leaf Lantern String | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Leaf Lantern String is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Noor and three gentle foxes hang a string of open leaf lanterns so fireflies may rest and leave whenever they wish. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Teamwork turns a tangled string into an open welcome.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
