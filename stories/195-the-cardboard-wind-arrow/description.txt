The Cardboard Wind Arrow is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Malik and Rita build a cardboard wind arrow to help them hang laundry where it will dry. The arrow sticks at first. With Auntie's help they loosen it safely, and then they test what they see against the cool breeze on their faces. The sheets dry, and their curiosity has a simple tool. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Curiosity asks what the wind is doing and then checks the feeling on your own cheek.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
