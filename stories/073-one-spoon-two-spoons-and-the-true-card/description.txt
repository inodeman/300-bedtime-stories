One Spoon, Two Spoons, and the True Card is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Nkechi writes two spoons of cinnamon instead of one, tastes the difference with Tomas and Hana, and corrects the card so both songs are honest. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Telling the truth about a mistake helps the next baker follow the right song.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
