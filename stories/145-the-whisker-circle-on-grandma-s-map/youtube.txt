The Whisker Circle on Grandma's Map | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Whisker Circle on Grandma's Map is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Hana follows Grandma's picture map of the lane, dock, and common, then draws a plumper letter box and a water bowl for small passersby. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A careful look can add a true new mark to an old map.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
