Quinn Asks for the Slow Tune | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Quinn Asks for the Slow Tune is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Quinn notices the musicians' walking tune is quicker than Nana's easy pace. Quinn gathers a breath and kindly asks them to play the slower song so the lantern walk can stay together. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can be a polite voice that helps everyone keep a comfortable pace.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
