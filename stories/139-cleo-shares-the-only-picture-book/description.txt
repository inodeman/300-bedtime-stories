Cleo Shares the Only Picture Book is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Cleo has the only picture book in the treehouse when two visitors want different pages, so she reads aloud and turns the pages slowly so everyone can see. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing a story by reading it aloud lets everyone have the same bright pages.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
