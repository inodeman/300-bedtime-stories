Bastian and the Right Snack for Ducks | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Bastian and the Right Snack for Ducks is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Bastian wants to feed the ducks a bag of old bread. He learns bread is not a healthy food for ducks. He does not dump it. Good slices become toast for people, and dry ends go into the compost. He brings oats and peas instead, and the ducks eat calmly at the pond. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Caring for nature means offering the right food and not wasting what we already have.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
