The Bridge Cloud and the Long Loaf | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Bridge Cloud and the Long Loaf is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Paloma feels grabby about the best cloud, then takes turns with Davin so the same sky can be a bridge, a loaf, and a mitten. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing a sky does not use it up.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
