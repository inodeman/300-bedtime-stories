Cora Mends the Torn Corner | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Cora Mends the Torn Corner is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Cora notices a small tear in a rabbit picture book. She and Ada hinge it with a soft paper strip, read the page gently, and leave the mended book ready for friends. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness includes caring for a book so the next reader finds it whole.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
