The Fern Picnic Blanket is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Lila and three gentle foxes each hold a corner of a wide fern picnic blanket until moss weights keep it flat. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Teamwork gives every corner a helper and every voice a vote.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
