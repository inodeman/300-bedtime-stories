The Orange That Kept Its Hidden Side | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Orange That Kept Its Hidden Side is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Petra wonders where the bitten part of the moon went, and Papa uses an orange and the lamp to show that the dim side is still there. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A bright edge can still belong to something whole.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
