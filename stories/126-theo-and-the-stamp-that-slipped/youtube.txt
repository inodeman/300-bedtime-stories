Theo and the Stamp That Slipped | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Theo and the Stamp That Slipped is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At the post office Theo finds a bright stamp that fell off a neighbor's letter, wants to keep its picture, and instead returns it so the letter can be mailed. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness is returning a small treasure so someone else's message can go on its way.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
