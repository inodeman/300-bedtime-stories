Pietro and the Honest Dot is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Pietro's elbow sends a blue drop onto Ines's chalk-path copy. He tells her at once. Together they lift a little color and turn the dot into a beetle with pencil legs. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Telling the truth right away keeps a small accident small and lets a fix begin.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
