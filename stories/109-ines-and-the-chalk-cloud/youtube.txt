Ines and the Chalk Cloud | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

Ines and the Chalk Cloud is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Kenji accidentally smudges Ines's chalk bird. They turn the smear into a smiling cloud and draw a path of birds and flowers that leads to the library steps. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A smudge can become a new idea when friends create together.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
