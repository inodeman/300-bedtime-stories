The Deep Green Hat for a Squinting Row | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Deep Green Hat for a Squinting Row is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Nia sees Femi squinting without his hat, offers a deep green one, and moves the hot watering can into the shade before they water the roots slowly. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness starts when you notice a squint and a thirsty can.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
