Lila and the Blue Creek Cup is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Lila finds a painted cup by the creek and wants to use it as a harbor for her leaf boat, then walks it back to the neighbor boy who lost it and shares the slow pool instead. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness means returning what you find, even when you wish you could keep it for a little while.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
