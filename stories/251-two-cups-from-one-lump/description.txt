Two Cups from One Lump is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Barak and Celine divide one lump of clay into two cups, trading pieces until the mounds feel fair. They share blue paint in turns and make a small dish from scraps. Both cups hold water, and neither one is ranked above the other. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing at the start keeps a project fair and friendly to the end.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
