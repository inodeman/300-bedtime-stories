The Leaf Ruler and the Surprising Puddle | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Leaf Ruler and the Surprising Puddle is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

Bjorn and Gracie measure puddle widths and depths with string, chalk, and a leaf ruler, and the narrow puddle turns out deeper than the wide one. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A fair measurement is worth more than a quick guess.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Three Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
